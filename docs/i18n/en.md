# PeMeO · English

**People first. Guided by good.**

## As technology moves forward, people stay at the heart.

A statement, an experience, a considered confirmation: each belongs to someone's life. We want technology to help you understand your life, retain your choices and stay connected to what matters.

Every product, project and technology we create should begin with real human needs, respect dignity and agency, and seek to benefit individuals, families and society.

1. Respect agency: explain consequential actions and their effects, and keep choices in human hands.
2. Respect people's words: distinguish statements, observations, inferences and confirmations; show sources and uncertainty.
3. Respect boundaries: minimize data collection and exposure; explain actual retention, sharing and deletion limits.
4. Make help accountable: ask who benefits and who might be harmed; explain mistakes, correct them and take responsibility.
5. Be honest about capability: separate aspirations, implemented features and verified results; welcome scrutiny and improvement.

PeMeO Core begins with clear records of statements, sources and confirmations. Original records use append-only storage; passkeys bind a confirmation action to specific content. Credential verification does not establish that the content is true. Administrators and the service process remain trusted.

An experimental developer-preview working draft, for evaluation with synthetic data. Complete personal memory, household assistance and a real AI inference entry point remain future work.

These principles guide design and collaboration. They add no Apache-2.0 usage restrictions, replace no security controls and do not claim that the system can automatically determine what is good.
